02 — THE APPROACH

Pretty is a start.
Useful is the point.

Every screen is a set of decisions.
Here’s the framework behind this collection.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Start with the task.

Identify what someone needs to accomplish, where they might hesitate, and what information helps them move forward.

02 / SIMPLIFY

Make the next step clear.

Build hierarchy around the primary action. Use progressive disclosure to keep detail available without overwhelming the screen.

03 / REFINE

Design the in-between.

Consider empty states, feedback, keyboard access, small screens, and reduced motion—not just the perfect first impression.

03 — UNDER THE SURFACE

One system.
Room to experiment.

Shared spacing, predictable controls, and a restrained palette make the details feel like they belong together.
